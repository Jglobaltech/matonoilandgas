### 1. Senior Pipeline Welding Inspector
*   **Job Title:** Senior Pipeline Welding Inspector
*   **Location:** Warri, Delta State (Onshore/Offshore)
*   **Job Type:** Full-time
*   **Job Description:**
    We are seeking a highly experienced Senior Pipeline Welding Inspector to oversee and verify welding quality, code compliance, and structural integrity during major pipeline laying and fabrication operations. The candidate will monitor pipeline construction, review weld radiographs, perform visual checks, and lead the welder qualification processes.
*   **Requirements & Qualifications:**
    Minimum of 8 years of active pipeline inspection experience.
    AWS Certified Welding Inspector (CWI) or CSWIP 3.1 credential.
    Strong working knowledge of ASME B31.4, B31.8, and API 1104 standards.
    Proficiency in non-destructive testing (NDT) methods interpretation.
*   **Application Instructions / Method:**
    Interested applicants should send their detailed CV and certifications to careers@matonog.com with the subject line "SENIOR WELDING INSPECTOR - WARRI".

### 2. Nondestructive Testing (NDT) Technician (Level II)
*   **Job Title:** NDT Technician (Level II)
*   **Location:** Port Harcourt, Rivers State
*   **Job Type:** Contract
*   **Job Description:**
    We are looking for certified NDT Level II Technicians to conduct visual, magnetic particle, dye penetrant, and ultrasonic testing on process piping, storage tanks, and structural components. The candidate is responsible for evaluating test results against international engineering codes, producing QA/QC reports, and ensuring field safety operations.
*   **Requirements & Qualifications:**
    Valid ASNT Level II certification in UT, MT, PT, and RT interpretation.
    At least 5 years of practical inspection experience in petrochemical or refinery facilities.
    Ability to read isometric drawings, P&IDs, and structural designs.
    Physical fitness suitable for climbing and working in confined spaces.
*   **Application Instructions / Method:**
    Submit your ASNT certifications, valid safety passport, and CV via our online portal or email to recruitment@matonog.com with code "NDT-L2-PH".

### 3. Offshore Drilling Rig Superintendent (Toolpusher)
*   **Job Title:** Drilling Rig Superintendent
*   **Location:** Niger Delta Basin (Offshore)
*   **Job Type:** Full-time
*   **Job Description:**
    The Rig Superintendent is responsible for the overall safety, efficiency, and operational execution of offshore exploratory and production drilling wells. Duties include managing crew rosters, coordinating subsea engineering tasks, optimizing mud weight schedules, troubleshooting rig machinery, and serving as the primary liaison with geological planning teams.
*   **Requirements & Qualifications:**
    Bachelor’s degree in Petroleum, Mechanical Engineering, or equivalent experience.
    Valid IWCF (International Well Control Forum) Level 4 Surface/Subsea Supervisor Certificate.
    10+ years of drilling experience, with a minimum of 4 years in a supervisory Toolpusher capacity.
    Exceptional crisis-management, incident response, and maritime safety leadership.
*   **Application Instructions / Method:**
    Apply by forwarding your qualifications and offshore credentials directly to chief-ops@matonog.com using subject line "OFFSHORE RIG SUPERINTENDENT APPLICATION".

### 4. HSE Coordinator (Health, Safety, and Environment)
*   **Job Title:** HSE Coordinator
*   **Location:** Lagos Headquarters / Site Visits
*   **Job Type:** Full-time
*   **Job Description:**
    The HSE Coordinator will champion our zero-incident culture by designing, implementing, and monitoring safety policies, fire prevention protocols, and environmental impact assessments at our shore bases and project offices. Key tasks include facilitating toolbox meetings, conducting site risk audits, maintaining safety logs, and leading incident root-cause investigations.
*   **Requirements & Qualifications:**
    NEBOSH International General Certificate (IGC) or equivalent professional safety diploma.
    5+ years of HSE experience in industrial fabrication, oil terminals, or logistics.
    Familiarity with ISO 14001 and ISO 45001 standards.
    Strong report-writing skills and fluency in safety compliance software tools.
*   **Application Instructions / Method:**
    Qualified candidates are invited to upload their CV and NEBOSH certificates to our careers page or send to hse-careers@matonog.com.
